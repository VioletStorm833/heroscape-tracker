Total = Atk × weight + Def × weight + Life × weight, using the weights in “Total formula” above (default 0.5 / 0.33 / 1, since a defense die and an attack die are each worth less than a point of life). Change them and every Total column, chart and army total updates. Per 100 points = stat × figures ÷ points × 100. Squads count every figure; the Crimson Twins share one life pool, so their life counts once. With specials adds always-on automatic skulls/shields and extra attacks that can all hit one target, plus bonuses whose condition is how the unit normally attacks (a ranged squad standing still to shoot). With conditional also adds situational self-bonuses: First Assault, Evasive, Assassinate, Melee Defense, terrain bonuses and squad-mate bonuses. Open a unit to see exactly what each mode counts.

* next to attack = an ability that hits several different figures (area or multi-target). Those dice are not added to attack, since they only pay off with several targets.

Dice odds

Each combat die has 6 sides: 3 skulls, 2 shields, 1 blank. Attacking counts skulls (1 in 2 per die), defending counts shields (1 in 3 per die). Every skull not blocked by a shield is a wound. The attack vs defense tools are on the Fight Simulator tab.

Fight to the finish

Two units fight in Heroscape rounds: each round both sides take 3 turns, alternating, starting with the side that wins initiative. They fight until one is wiped out. Every figure still alive at the start of its side’s turn attacks once (destroyed squad figures stop attacking for the rest of the fight), one figure at a time defends, and extra wounds past a figure's life are lost. With a bonded unit, both units attack every turn (as bonding lets them act together), and attacks hit the main unit first, then the bonded unit. Results come from 1,000,000 simulated fights. Click any result box to see its graph.
